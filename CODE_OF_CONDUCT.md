# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Participate constructively,
assume good intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and the public integration need.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is inconsistent with this commitment. Report conduct concerns privately to
`tyler@reptilian.dev`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related public
or private communication.
